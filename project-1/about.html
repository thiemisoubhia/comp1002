The Super Arno: The Hairdryer That Defined an Era

Unprecedented Power. Unforgettable Volume. The 1960s Icon, Faithfully Recreated.

The Legacy of Volume

In the early 1960s, hairstyles soared to new heights. The French Twist, the Bouffant, and the Beehive demanded tools capable of delivering controlled power and precise setting to achieve that iconic, gravity-defying shape. The original Arno Super was that indispensable tool—a styling revolution in the home.

The Arno Heritage re-edition is not a replica, but a resurrection. We utilized original archival designs to recreate the aesthetic down to the last curve, while engineering the internals to meet modern safety and efficiency standards.

Detailed Signature Features: Built for the Bouffant!

Turbo-Flow Motor

Equipped with an optimized 400-watt motor, delivering the powerful, consistent airflow necessary for rapid drying and maximum lift—just like the original that women relied on in 1965.

Precision Styling Settings

Features simple Low and High temperature controls for versatile styling. Use High for quick bulk drying and Low for precision setting, ensuring your finished style is locked in place.

Iconic Design & Finish

Available in a chic range of original pastel colors to match your vanity, including Mint Green, Powder Blue, and Dusty Rose. A true piece of functional 1960s art.
