Arno Heritage Collection: Corporate Overview

Our Vision and Values

The Arno Heritage Collection operates as a dedicated division focused on the meticulous re-edition of iconic Arno appliances. Our vision is to preserve and celebrate mid-century design excellence while ensuring products meet modern standards of performance and reliability.

  • Authenticity: Commitment to original design blueprints.
  • Quality: Utilizing robust materials and engineering.
  • Heritage: Valuing the rich history of the Arno brand.

Growth and Market Strategy

We target the premium collector and home décor markets, positioning our products as durable, investment-quality pieces. Our strategy focuses on controlled, low-volume production to maintain exclusivity and design integrity.

Future Outlook: We are continuously researching archival designs with the goal of expanding the Heritage line to include other defining appliances from the 1950s and 70s.
